Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Family Tree ("the app") is a free tool for visualising and managing family tree data stored in a Google Sheet that belongs to you.

Data We Collect

The app has no servers or database of its own and does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data. Your family tree data stays in your own Google Sheet. The app reads it directly from Google and displays it in your browser.

Google Account Sign-In

Viewing a tree does not require signing in. If you choose to sign in to edit, the app uses Google Sign-In to get a temporary access token. It requests:

  • Access to files you create or select with the app (Google Drive "drive.file" permission). The app can only see sheets that it created for you, or that you picked yourself in Google's file picker. It cannot see any other files in your Google Drive.
  • Your email address, only to show which account is signed in and to check whether that account may edit the tree.

The access token is kept only in your browser, never on any server. To keep you signed in when you reload the page, it is saved in your browser's local storage until it expires (about one hour) or you sign out. We never see your Google password.

What the App Does With This Access

  • Reads and updates the people in your family tree sheet when you add, edit, or remove someone.
  • When you use "Create my tree", creates a new Google Sheet in your own Google Drive and sets it to "Anyone with the link can view", so the tree can be opened from a link.
  • When you choose "Fix it for me" on a sheet that isn't shared yet, sets that sheet to "Anyone with the link can view".
  • Checks whether your account is allowed to edit the sheet. Only people the sheet's owner added as Editors can make changes.
  • Lists the family tree sheets you created or opened with the app, so you can find them again on any device after signing in.
  • Keeps your trees together in a "Freemily Trees" folder in your Google Drive: new trees are created there, and sheets you open with the app get a shortcut there (the sheet itself is not moved).

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from your Google account is used only to provide the features described above, is not transferred to anyone else, and is never used for advertising.

Cookies & Local Storage

The app saves your sheet's ID (and, if you use the advanced options, your own Client ID or Apps Script URL) in your browser's local storage so you don't have to enter it again. It also remembers your place in the setup guide. This data never leaves your device.

Removing Access

You can remove the app's access at any time in your Google account permissions. Your sheet stays in your Google Drive; delete it there if you no longer want it.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Google services: Google Identity Services (sign-in), the Google Sheets API, the Google Drive API, and the Google Picker. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles data.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue at the project repository or contact the site owner.
